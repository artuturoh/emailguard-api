Quick Integration

Send an email to POST /v1/verify and receive immediate intelligence.

request.sh 
curl --request POST \
  --url 'https://emailguard-disposable-email-detector-and-hygiene.p.rapidapi.com/v1/verify' \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --header 'X-RapidAPI-Key: YOUR_RAPIDAPI_KEY' \
  --header 'X-RapidAPI-Host: emailguard-disposable-email-detector-and-hygiene.p.rapidapi.com' \
  --data '{ "email": "juan.perez@gmial.com" }'
